fa-rclone privacy policy

fa-rclone is used only by its owner, to access the owner's own Google Drive files from the owner's own server.

  • It does not collect data from anyone else.
  • Files it reads from Google Drive stay on the owner's server and are not shared, sold or sent to third parties.
  • The owner can revoke its access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
